Korea Nazarene University International Language Institute has organized a systematic Korean
language curriculumso that foreigners and overseasKoreans can studyKorean and experience
Korean culture.
Integrated learning includes speaking,listening,reading andwriting,with afocusonpractical commu-
nication skills. Our goal is to improve Korean communication skills focusing on real life,as well as
Korean language skillsfor undergraduate and graduate school.We provide opportunities to experience
various aspects of Korean culture to help international students have a succesfullearning experience
in Korea, and help them choose the right major through systematic college admissions counseling.
