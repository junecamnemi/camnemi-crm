Transfer Admission
- For entry into the 2nd year: Applicants must be graduates of a junior college or have completed the first year (2 semesters, excluding seasonal

- For entry into the 4th year:: Applicants must be graduates of a three-year college program or have completed the third year (6 semesters, excluding 
seasonal semesters) of a four-year university program.

 -Final successful applicants must submit a new original bank balance certificate that meets the visa issuance requirements

Applicant Notes
1. Dual Enrollment Prohibition
After all admission procedures are complete, it is prohibited to register at more than one university, industrial university, education college, or junior college for the 
same academic term.
2. Important Applicant Notes
 1)If the information provided in the application is different from the facts, or if there are errors or omissions in the provided details, the applicant is responsible 
for any disadvantages that arise.
 2)If it is confirmed that the applicant does not meet the qualifications, has falsified documents, or has engaged in any fraudulent acts, their admission may be 
canceled, even after enrollment. 
3)Once the application is submitted, no changes or cancellations are allowed, and the application fee is non-refundable. Submitted documents will not be 
returned.
 4)The applicant must provide accurate email and phone contact information that the university can use to reach them (the contact details of study abroad 
agencies or language schools cannot be used). If the applicant cannot be contacted due to incorrect or missing contact details, the responsibility lies with the 
applicant. 
 All international students must obtain a TOPIK Level 4 (Level 3 for arts and physical education majors) 
before graduation. Students in the bilingual track must obtain TOPIK Level 2 or pass the SU-TOPIK 
Level 2 test, or complete Level 2 of the Korean language course at Sahmyook University’s Korean 
Language Institute before graduation.
Submission Document Guidelines
1. Documents must be completed and submitted within the submission period.
2.  If there are any issues with the details entered in the application form or if the submitted documents are incomplete or there is a failure in 
communication, the applicant may be excluded from the evaluation process, and the applicant is responsible for any disadvantages resulting from 
this.
3.  If there are discrepancies in the names on the submitted documents, you must attach a certificate proving that the different names refer 
to the same person, issued by the relevant national court or consulate.
4.  All documents not written in Korean or English must be translated into Korean and notarized (within three months of the submission date)..
5.  Prospective graduates must submit the final academic certificate and transcript, which includes information about their final semester, by the additional 
document submission deadline. Failure to submit these documents may result in admission cancellation.
6.  If any documents are found to be falsified or fraudulent, admission will be canceled, and the tuition paid will not be refunded.
(However, if the semester in which the tuition was paid has not yet ended, refunds will be provided according to the university’s tuition refund policy.)
Admission Procedure & Applicant Notes
5)If contact information changes during the admission process and the applicant fails to notify the International Education Center, resulting in 
communication issues, the applicant will be held responsible for any disadvantages.
6)The scores and evaluation details will not be disclosed.
7)If any document falsification, fraud, or qualification issues are discovered during the verification process, or if the applicant is denied a 
visa by immigration authorities, the applicant will be disqualified and admission canceled. In such cases, the tuition fees may not be fully 
refunded.
8)Any matters not specified will be decided by the 2025 Foreign Student Degree Program Admissions Committee.
