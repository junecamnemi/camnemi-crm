A one-on-one customized Korean language assistant program to improve learning

The Korean Language Training Program is designed for learners who wish to study Korean
intensively in order to enter Korean universities or graduate schools, or to pursue careers
or business opportunities related to Korea. The program is offered four times a year
(Spring, Summer, Fall, and Winter), allowing learners to choose the most suitable term to
begin their studies. Classes are divided into Levels 1 through 6 according to students'
Korean proficiency.
