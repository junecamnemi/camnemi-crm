Established in 2002, Yeungnam University's Korean and International Language Institute (KILI) serves a
large number of students from all over the world with dedicated teachers who hold the 1st or the 2nd level
license in Korean language teaching.
KLI offers 4 semesters a year, with each semester taking 10 weeks to complete 200 hours of instruction.
