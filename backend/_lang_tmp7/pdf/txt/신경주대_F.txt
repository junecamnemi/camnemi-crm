Course Introduction

As a systematic and intensive Korean language course for foreigners who want to study abroad and practice their Korean, the regular term program
provides integrated education of communication oriented speaking, listening, reading and writing. The program aims for not only improving communicative
competence based on real life, but also improving Korean language skills needed for entering school.

Application Process

1) Submit documents
2) Document examination
3) Receive notice of success in admission and tuition fee invoice
4) Tuition fee payment
5) Receive admission letter
6) Apply for visa
7) Receive visa
8) Start of semester @SGU

- The time required from submitting admission documents to obtaining a visa varies by the country of residence, so you must check the visa information
at the diplimatic missions of South Korea in advance.
