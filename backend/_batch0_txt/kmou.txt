Ⅶ
Additional Information
1. The applicant is responsible for any penalties such as cancellation of admission 
due to incomplete document submission, errors or omissions in the application 
form, omission of or errors in contact information or postal address (contact 
immediately if there are any changes during the selection process), failure to 
check the announcement of successful candidates, or being unreachable.
2. If the applicant is found to have been admitted to the university by fraudulent 
means, such as forgery or alteration of application documents, their admission 
will be canceled, and their enrollment will be terminated. 
3. Submitted documents will not be returned under any circumstances; however, 
original documents such as graduation certificates, which cannot be re-issued, 
will be returned after the selection process is complete.
4. Once the application has been submitted, no changes can be made to the division 
(department) or selection type, and the application cannot be canceled.
5.  Applicants may apply for only one admission unit at the university.
6. Applicants must bring their identification card (foreigner residence card, passport, 
etc.) and application ticket to the interview.
7. Successful applicants must read the  "Guidelines for Successful Applicants" on 
the university's website and pay the tuition fee accordingly. If an applicant does 
not register within the designated period, they will be deemed to have no 
intention of enrolling  and their admission will be canceled.
8. If an applicant wishes to receive a refund for the tuition fee, they must cancel the 
course registration and apply for a refund through the university's admissions website.
9. Matters not specified in the admission guideline shall be subject to the decision 
of the university's Admissions Management Committee.
10. Transfers between schools offering the same degree program in Korea may be 
restricted based on the total duration of stay; therefore, applicants should  
inquire about this before applying.
11. The admission guidelines are provided in both Korean and English. In case of 
any discrepancies,  the Korean version shall prevail. 
12. If a student is enrolled in two or more universities simultaneously within the  
same admission period, their acceptance and enrollment will be canceled.
13. If visa issuance is rejected by the Foreign Ministry's overseas missions or the 
Ministry of Justice's Immigration and Foreign Affairs Office, the acceptance will 
be canceled, and the tuition fee will be refunded.

<Important Notes on Submitting the Seafarer's Medical Certificate>
1. The medical examination must be conducted at a general hospital or, higher-level 
medical institution, or a specialized medical institution registered with the local 
Regional Office of Oceans and Fisheries.
2. The seafarer's medical examination must include a special health examination.
3. All required examination items must be completed, and the declaration must be 
fully filled out by a licensed specialist.
4. In the section for the examining physician's name and signature, the full name of 
the physician must be written and signed.
5. The name of the issuing medical institution must be stated and officially stamped or sealed.
6. The medical certificate must have been issued within 3 months of the submission date.
7. Medical certificates issued by foreign medical institutions will be accepted only if 
they are notarized in accordance with the above requirements.
8. Additional Notes:
  - The issuance period for the medical certificate may vary depending on the region 
and medical institution. Please verify this in advance.
  - The medical certificate must be submitted within the designated submission period.
9. Guidance Video on Submitting the Medical Certificate: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DfE1TZ2U5Ug&t=2s
