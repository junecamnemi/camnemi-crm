Qualifications
1. Nationality
Both the applicant and the parents must be foreign nationals
- If the applicant and the parents acquired foreign nationality through emigration or adoption, their acquisition must have been 
completed before the applicant started the curricula equivalent to Korea’s elementary and secondary education in a foreign 
country (the proof of nationality acquisition is required).
※ Korean and dual nationals are not eligible to apply.
※ Both the applicant and the parents must complete the renunciation or loss of Korean nationality before the application deadline.
※ Ground:「Enforcement Decree of the Higher Education Act」Article 29 Paragraph 2 No. 6
2. Language Proficiency
※ ‌Applicant who is studying at PNU Language Education Institute should submit the certificate of enrollment. If the applicant fails to 
submit language proficiency (completion of PNU LEI) by Jan 30, 2026, Friday, preliminary admission shall be canceled.
※ ‌Applicants with Nepalese nationality must have a language proficiency score of TOPIK level 5, IELTS 6.5, or TOEFL iBT 94 
or higher, regardless of the department’s general language requirements
